## **PART 6: SALES & MARKETING SECURITY (10 Slides)**

**Slide 1: You Sell Trust Before You Sell Products**
* "Every deal represents customer trust. Lose it once — it's gone forever"
* Handle sensitive client info: emails, contracts, proposals, campaign data
* Common threats: fake clients, compromised CRM, exposed spreadsheets

**Slide 2: Customer & Lead Data Protection**
| Data Type | Example | Secure Handling |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Personal Identifiers | Name, phone, email | Store in company CRM only |
| Financial/Contract | Quotes, invoices | Official email/portal only |
| Marketing Analytics | Campaign reports | Secured dashboards |
| Prospect Lists | Lead spreadsheets | Encrypt and limit access |

**Slide 3: Secure CRM & Lead Management**
* **Login Access:** Enable MFA on Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho
* **Sharing Data:** Role-based access — avoid "Public View"
* **Data Cleanup:** Delete old leads per retention policy
* **Export Control:** Only authorized managers can export
* **Audit Logs:** Regularly review CRM access logs
* **Never export entire CRM lists to Excel/Gmail/WhatsApp**

**Slide 4: Phishing & Impersonation Scams**
* #1 attack on sales teams: fake clients
* **Tactics:** Spoofed client emails, malicious RFP links, fake event invites, CEO impersonation
* **Defense:** Verify sender domain, hover before clicking, confirm large requests via phone, use approved email templates, report immediately

**Slide 5: Social Media & Brand Security**
* "One careless post can reveal company strategy"
* **LinkedIn:** Don't post client names, contracts, screenshots
* **Instagram/Facebook:** Blur badges, systems, internal data
* **Email Campaigns:** Don't use open/public mailing lists
* **Hashtags/Campaigns:** Avoid sensitive keywords (#ConfidentialDeal)
* **Vendor Collaboration:** Check permissions before co-marketing

**Slide 6: Email & Document Sharing Hygiene**
| Action | Do | Don't |
|--------|----|-------|
| Sending Proposals | Official domain, password-protect PDFs | Use Gmail, WhatsApp |
| File Sharing | SharePoint, OneDrive, company Drive | WeTransfer, Dropbox personal |
| Email Attachments | Double-check recipient | Mass CCs without consent |
| Contact Lists | Encrypt exports | Store locally on laptop |
| Marketing Reports | Redact PII before sharing | Publish full datasets |

**Slide 7: Marketing Campaign Compliance**
| Law | Applies To | Responsibility |
|-----|------------|---------------|
| GDPR (EU) | EU citizen data | Consent before marketing emails |
| DPDP Act (India) | Indian user data | Protect data, allow opt-out |
| CAN-SPAM (US) | Email marketing | Include unsubscribe link |
| ISO 27001 | Org-wide security | Approved handling & storage |

**Slide 8: Data Leak & Incident Response**
* If you accidentally leak customer info:
  1. Don't delete or hide it
  2. Report to IT/Security immediately
  3. Inform supervisor with full details
  4. IT may revoke access, reset credentials
  5. Cooperate in remediation and training
* **Fast reporting = damage control. Silence = escalation**

**Slide 9: Sales & Marketing Security Checklist**
- [ ] Protect customer data like money
- [ ] Verify sender and client identity before sharing
- [ ] Use company-approved channels for proposals
- [ ] Enable MFA on all CRM and marketing platforms
- [ ] Get consent before marketing emails
- [ ] Think before you post — protect brand reputation
- [ ] Encrypt sensitive files before sharing
- [ ] Regularly review CRM access logs
- [ ] Report suspicious emails immediately
- [ ] Follow data retention policies

**Slide 10: Sales & Marketing Key Takeaways**
* Customer trust is your most valuable asset
* Verify identity before sharing any information
* Use approved tools — avoid personal email/cloud
* MFA protects your CRM and marketing accounts
* Compliance is not optional — it's mandatory
* Your online presence reflects company security
