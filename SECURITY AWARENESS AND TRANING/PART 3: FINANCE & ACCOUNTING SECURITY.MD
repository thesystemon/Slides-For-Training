## **PART 3: FINANCE & ACCOUNTING SECURITY (10 Slides)**

**Slide 1: Cybercriminals Love Finance Teams**
* #1 target for Business Email Compromise (BEC)
* Real case: ₹1.7 crore lost to fake invoice scam
* 83% of financial frauds start with phishing (FBI IC3 Report, 2024)

**Slide 2: Common Threats Targeting Finance**
* Invoice Fraud (spoofed vendor emails)
* Payment Diversion Scams (changed bank details)
* Business Email Compromise (CEO/CFO spoofing)
* Phishing/Smishing (fake payment portal links)
* Payroll Fraud (diverted salary payments)

**Slide 3: How Invoice Fraud Happens**
1. Attacker compromises/spoofs vendor email
2. Sends fake invoice with changed account details
3. Uses urgent tone: "Payment pending! Late fees apply!"
4. Finance pays to wrong account → money gone
* **Once payment is made, reversal is almost impossible**

**Slide 4: How to Spot Fake Invoices**
* ✉️ Email mismatch (@vend0r.com vs @vendor.com)
* 📁 Unusual file types (.html, .exe, .zip)
* 💸 Changed bank details (new IFSC/account number)
* ⏰ Urgency pressure ("Pay today or late fee!")
* 🗣️ Unverified sender (not in official vendor list)
* 🧾 Inconsistent format (old logo, grammar errors)

**Slide 5: Business Email Compromise (BEC) Defense**
| Attack Type | Example | Defense |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| CEO Fraud | Fake CEO demanding urgent transfer | Verify via phone/Teams |
| Vendor Spoofing | Looks like vendor.invoices@gmail.com | Use company directory |
| Mailbox Compromise | Attacker reads invoice chains | Enable MFA on all accounts |
| Payment Diversion | Bank details swapped | Confirm details before every payment |

**Slide 6: Secure Payment & Accounting Practices**
* Use MFA on all finance portals (ERP, SAP, Tally)
* Dual authorization for payments above ₹50,000
* Never process email-only payment instructions
* Verify vendor bank changes with phone confirmation
* Isolate payroll/finance systems from internet browsing
* Don't reuse finance passwords on personal accounts

**Slide 7: Cloud, ERP & Email Security**
* **ERP Access:** Use VPN/SSO, never login via public Wi-Fi
* **Email Security:** Official domain, enable SPF/DKIM/DMARC
* **Data Sharing:** Encrypted Excel/PDF with password
* **Backup & Recovery:** Daily backup to secure drives
* **Remote Work:** VPN mandatory, disable auto-forward

**Slide 8: Payroll & Salary Security**
| Risk | Example | Defense |
|------|---------|---------|
| Fake HR Email | "Change employee salary account" | Verify with HR directly |
| Insider Theft | Payroll admin copies PANs | Access control & logging |
| Salary Data Leak | Spreadsheet shared externally | Encrypt or use HRMS only |
| Portal Compromise | Weak credentials | MFA + strong passwords |

**Slide 9: Finance Security Checklist**
- [ ] Verify all vendor bank changes via phone
- [ ] Enable MFA on finance and ERP logins
- [ ] Never approve payment via email alone
- [ ] Keep finance PCs isolated from casual browsing
- [ ] Encrypt financial files before sharing
- [ ] Conduct monthly vendor data verification
- [ ] Review finance system access logs weekly
- [ ] Avoid public Wi-Fi for online payments
- [ ] Use official invoice templates only
- [ ] Report suspicious payment requests immediately

**Slide 10: Finance Security Key Takeaways**
* Finance teams are prime targets — vigilance is your firewall
* Always verify bank changes via call or vendor portal
* Never rush payments based on urgency or "CEO requests"
* Protect login credentials — enable MFA everywhere
* Treat all invoices as potential phishing until verified
* Build a "4-eye rule" — two people approve every payment
